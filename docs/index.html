Built for how artists really work

Everything here is in the free download.

Infinite canvasImages, GIFs, video, audio, 3D, PDFs and notes on one board. Pan, zoom, align, annotate. Map ViewSee every reference and its connections as a real node graph. Drag ports to relate cards. Scratch BoardA freeform sketch and notes layer that lives with each board. Task trackerPer-board checklists next to the work they belong to. Layers & propertiesHide, lock and rename cards. Fine control over position, size and color. Import & exportOpen PureRef files, drop in almost anything, export PNG, PDF or a shareable board.

Stays beside your work

Always on top over ZBrush, Blender and Photoshop, even apps that also float. MoodLock hides every toolbar and shows just the picture, with Left and Right to flip through references and a window that remembers where you parked it.

How Always on top, Top Mode and MoodLock work →

  • Always on top, re-raised automatically
  • Top Mode: floating, minimal chrome
  • MoodLock: read-only, picture only
  • Presentation mode for clients

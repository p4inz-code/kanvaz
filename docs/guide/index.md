## What you get

- **An infinite canvas** for images, video, audio, 3D models, PDFs, notes and PureRef boards.
- **Map View**: wire references together and see them as a real graph.
- **100% offline**: no account, no cloud, no subscription, nothing leaves your machine.
- **Stays beside your work**: always on top over ZBrush, Blender and Photoshop, plus [MoodLock](working-with-other-apps.html) for a clean, distraction-free reference.
- **Plugins**: four official ones, and you can write your own.

## Stay in the loop

Kanvaz ships fixes and features often, and every release is written up in the [changelog](https://github.com/p4inz-code/kanvaz/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

- On GitHub choose **Watch → Custom → Releases** to hear about new versions and nothing else.
- Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/8UKt8s5FbW) to share boards, ask questions and vote on what gets built next.
- Missing something? [Request a feature](https://github.com/p4inz-code/kanvaz/issues/new). Every request gets read.
- Kanvaz is free and stays free. If it saves you time you can [support development](https://p4inz-code.github.io/donate/), and a [star on GitHub](https://github.com/p4inz-code/kanvaz) helps other artists find it.
